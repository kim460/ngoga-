A Passionate Creator & Academic Achiever from Rwanda

I am a highly motivated and accomplished student with a proven record of academic excellence, leadership, and creative innovation. Currently pursuing the Mathematics, Economics, and Computer Science (MCE) combination, I maintain a strong GPA of 3.83, reflecting my consistency, discipline, and analytical ability.

My achievements include earning the Gold Medal at the East African Economics Olympiad (2025), where I attained the highest overall average among participants from multiple countries. I was recognized as Best in Economics and Financial Literacy at the regional level and led my school's Economics Club to a national victory at the Rwanda-Kenya Economic Olympiad.

Beyond academics, I am deeply engaged in videography, photography, and digital storytelling. As Vice President of the Media Club, I've helped increase school social media engagement from 300 to over 12,000 viewers.
